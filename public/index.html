UNO AIRDOSA - Game Rules

Objective

The main objective is to be the first player to discard all of the cards in your hand.

Card Types

  • Number Cards (0-9): Standard cards in Red, Yellow, Green, and Blue.
  • Skip Card: The next player in the current play direction loses their turn.
  • Reverse Card: Reverses the direction of play. Clockwise becomes Counter-Clockwise and vice versa.
  • Draw Two (+2): The next player draws 2 cards and misses their turn.
  • Wild Card: Allows the player to choose the next active color in play.
  • Wild Draw Four (+4): Allows the player to choose the next color AND forces the next player to draw 4 cards and lose their turn.

Special Room Variations (Configurable)

  • Stacking draws: If a +2 card is played, the next player can stack another +2 card instead of drawing. The accumulated penalty transfers to the subsequent player. The same stacking rule applies to +4 cards.
  • Jump-In rule: If a player holds a card that matches the top discard pile card in BOTH color AND symbol/value, they may play it instantly, even out of turn. The play flow resumes from their seat.
  • Seven-Zero rule: Playing a number 7 card forces that player to swap their entire hand with an opponent of their choice. Playing a number 0 card rotates everyone's hand in the active play direction.

UNO Shout Rules

When you have only one card left in your hand, you must press the UNO button. If you forget to do so before another player calls you out (by pressing the Call Out alert), you will receive a penalty of 2 cards.
